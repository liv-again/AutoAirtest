# Market Index Rules

证券行情指数验证规则。

## Order Rules

- Domestic index order: 上证指数, 深证成指, 北证50, 科创综指.
- Detail order with industry section: 行业板块, 上证指数, 深证成指, 科创综指, 北证50, 创业板指.
- Detail order without industry section: 上证指数, 深证成指, 科创综指, 北证50, 创业板指.

## Boundaries

Do not make final claims about market data correctness. Numeric quotes, 涨跌幅, 成交额, and cross-terminal consistency require human review.

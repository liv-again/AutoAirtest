# Airtest Poco Operations

Airtest/Poco 执行动作技能，用于把规划动作映射为低风险设备操作。

## Operations

- Prefer Poco semantic locators for text, description, and name attributes.
- Use Airtest screenshots before and after high-value actions.
- Keep swipe, input, back, and tap actions auditable through execution traces.

## Boundaries

Sensitive evidence may be redacted. Never treat redacted screenshots or UI trees as proof of final market data correctness.
